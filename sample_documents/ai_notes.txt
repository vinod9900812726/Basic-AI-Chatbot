
Artificial Intelligence and Generative AI

Artificial Intelligence is a branch of computer science
that develops systems capable of performing tasks that
normally require human intelligence.

Machine Learning is a subset of Artificial Intelligence.
It allows computers to learn patterns from data.

Deep Learning uses neural networks with multiple layers
to learn complex patterns.

Generative AI creates new content such as text, images,
audio, video, and computer code.

Large Language Models are trained on large collections
of text and can generate responses to user prompts.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation combines information
retrieval with language generation. It retrieves relevant
information from documents and provides that information
to a language model as context.

Embeddings represent text as numerical vectors.
Semantically similar text can have similar embeddings.

Vector similarity search helps retrieve document passages
that are relevant to a user's question.

Python is widely used for AI development because of its
libraries for machine learning, data processing, and NLP.
